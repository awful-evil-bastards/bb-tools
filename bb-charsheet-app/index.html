⚔ Permanent Scars
Scars are earned by failing Death Saves (failed roll auto-adds a random scar) or assigned by the GM. Each scar is a lasting mark — usually impossible to remove without rare magic or a heroic quest.
🜲 Afflictions 0 / 6
Afflictions are earned by failing Resolve checks against Madness sources (eldritch horrors, Black Magic, forbidden knowledge, the unbearable witnessed). They are the mind's analog of Permanent Scars. At 6 Afflictions the next failure makes the character Lost — removed from play, retired narratively. Sanity Vigil can attempt to remove one.

💥 Badassery Dice (Meta-Currency)
0 d6 Max 3 • Earned by being badass
Add die to a failed d20 roll. Carries over between sessions. Spend up to 3 on one roll if desperate.
🤝 Saga Dice (Team-Ups)
0 d6 Max 3 • Gifted by fellow players (1/session each)
Spend on your turn: one combined move with a willing ally — one roll w/ advantage by the payload-bearer; the Saga die rides the to-hit and always counts as Spillover (even on Cautious/Defensive moves; adds to effect if non-damage). Ally's part costs their reaction unless a 2nd Saga die is spent — which buys another die on the beat OR negates a Greater Foe's Inevitable Reaction. Bank unspent as XP at session's end.
