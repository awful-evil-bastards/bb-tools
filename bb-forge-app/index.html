BICEPS & BRIMSTONE

Character Forge

1 · Ancestry

The blood in your veins and the culture that raised you — it sets your size, speed, and one ability nudge.

2 · Class

What you trained to become. Sets your Hit Point base, resource dice, and signature weapon.

3 · Backgrounds

Pick two: one required by your class, one from any list. Each grants 3–5 signature skills (see §4).

Backgrounds grant specific skills (with the occasional “pick one”). You’ll mark them in Step 6.

4 · Ability Scores

Generate six scores, then your ancestry bonuses apply on top.

5 · Derived Stats

Calculated from your class and scores. Pick your armor to finish your AC.

6 · Skills

Your class signature weapon is locked in. Check the skills your two backgrounds grant, plus any extras.

7 · Starting Kit & Powers

What every fresh hero begins play with.

Adventurer’s Kit · 10 rations · 50 coin · your class weapons & armor.

8 · Identity

Name your hero and write the three declarations that earn roleplay XP.
